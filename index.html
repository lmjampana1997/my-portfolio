Featured work

The Sewing Tin NYC
— a case study

The Sewing Tin is a nomadic culinary studio and supper club founded by chefs Akhil Upad and Aditya Mishra. Named after the iconic Danish cookie tin repurposed into a sewing kit in South Asian homes, the brand is rooted in desi nostalgia, resourcefulness, and communal dining. Six courses. Four cocktails. Thirty seats. Select Sundays in NYC.

I manage their social media presence across Instagram and TikTok, developing content strategy, writing copy, directing and editing Reels, and building the visual language that took them from a Hell's Kitchen apartment dinner to a sold-out nomadic culinary studio with a devoted following.
